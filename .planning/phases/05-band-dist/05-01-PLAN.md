<objective>
Add dynamic per-ticket band distribution to the wheeling system. User selects a band scheme (preset or custom) from the sidebar, and the wheeling algorithm produces tickets that match that scheme. Each ticket's band composition is displayed in the summary and volantes.

Purpose: Currently, the pool has a band distribution (Baja/Media/Alta counts) but the wheeling algorithm generates tickets without any per-ticket band constraint. This means a ticket could have all 10 numbers from one band. Phase 5 adds the ability to constrain each ticket to follow a specific B-M-A distribution.

Output: Modified `app.py` with: (1) sidebar band distribution selector with presets + custom, (2) uniform/variable toggle, (3) pool validation for per-ticket distribution, (4) band-filtered wheeling algorithm, (5) per-ticket band composition display.
</objective>
